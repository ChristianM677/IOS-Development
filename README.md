# IOS-Development
These are a couple of apps I made while learning and following a course on IOS Development. 

## IOS Apps
- BMI Calculator
- Clima
- Hacker News
- Quizzler
- Tipsy

## BMI Calculator

This is a pretty straightforward app which takes your height and weight to calculate the BMI. This app functions with sliders which allow you to slide to your weight and height and then when pressing the calculate button you're met with another screen which shows the results and a useful note.

## Clima

This app is a weather app that catches the real time weather data using an API website called OpenWeather. Its pretty straightforward where you can search for a city and it will give you the weather with its icon. 

## Hacker News

This is an app that depends solely on an API website and this app grabs the top Technology or hack news trending right now and you are able to go further by going to the official article of it.

## Quizzler

This app is just a fun quizzing game where you have to answer true or false questions and most of the questions are random and weird to be true. The app also has a scoring system.

## Tipsy

This app helps you split how much of a total bill each person will pay. By adding the bill total, the percentage tip and the amount of people the app does the math and gives you the pay per person.
